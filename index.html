BUILT FOR FIGHTERS.

Roar Mixed Martial Arts has been forging fighters and transforming everyday athletes in Lahore since 2011. Led by federation-certified head coach Azhar Hussain, we train across Boxing, Kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA, Wushu, and Self-Defense — alongside strength, conditioning, and weight-loss programs for every age and level. From your very first class to the competition cage, you train with discipline, focus, and a coach who has done it at the highest level.

Located in Block G, Canal Gardens, our facility is a fully-equipped combat sports floor — matted training areas, heavy bags, speed bags, a dedicated strength & conditioning zone, and a women's self-defense space. Train in-club five days a week, join special weekend sessions, or book one-on-one and home personal training.
